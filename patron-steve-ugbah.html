Meet Our Patron: Ambassador Steve Torkuma Ugbah — A Pillar of Leadership, Service, and Inspiration

Published: 11 October 2025 · Author: BBCSI

The Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative (BBCSI) proudly celebrates our esteemed Patron, Ambassador Steve Torkuma Ugbah, a distinguished Nigerian academic, diplomat, and public servant whose remarkable journey continues to inspire service, integrity, and excellence.

Ambassador Ugbah’s story is one of dedication to education, leadership, and national development. He earned his Ph.D. in Communication from Ohio University and went on to become an Emeritus Professor in the Department of Marketing & Entrepreneurship at California State University, East Bay. His decades-long academic career reflects a passion for learning, mentorship, and the empowerment of future leaders.

Before venturing into diplomacy, Prof. Ugbah made his mark in Nigeria’s political landscape, running for the governorship of Benue State in 2011. His courage and commitment to democratic principles were evident throughout his campaign and the subsequent electoral litigation that reached the Supreme Court. Through it all, his vision for a more equitable and progressive Benue remained steadfast.

In 2017, Ugbah’s expertise and integrity earned him the prestigious appointment as Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to the Russian Federation, with concurrent accreditation to the Republic of Belarus. In this role, he worked tirelessly to strengthen Nigeria’s bilateral relations with Russia, fostering collaborations in trade, culture, and humanitarian development. He also championed the protection and welfare of Nigerian citizens abroad, embodying the true spirit of diplomacy and patriotism.

In August 2024, Ambassador Ugbah took on yet another significant national responsibility as Federal Commissioner for the Revenue Mobilisation, Allocation & Fiscal Commission (RMFAC), representing Benue State. His appointment reaffirms his reputation as a trusted leader, reform advocate, and servant of the people.

The Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative (BBCSI) is deeply honored to have Ambassador Steve Torkuma Ugbah as our Patron. His unwavering commitment to public service and community development aligns perfectly with our mission to create awareness, support patients, and promote early detection in the fight against breast cancer.

Ambassador Ugbah’s life and achievements remind us that true leadership is measured not just by positions held, but by lives touched and communities uplifted. His guidance and partnership continue to strengthen BBCSI’s mission to build a healthier, more informed society.

We celebrate a visionary leader, an educator, and a humanitarian — Ambassador Steve Torkuma Ugbah.
