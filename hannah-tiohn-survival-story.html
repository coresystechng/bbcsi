My Breast Cancer Survival Story — By Hannah Mngohol Tiohn

Published: 11 October 2025
Author: Hannah Mngohol Tiohn

Hello, my name is Hannah Mngohol Tiohn, and I am the Chairman of the Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative (BBCSI). My journey with breast cancer began in 2012, when I was diagnosed with the disease — a moment that changed my life forever.

I began treatment in May 2012 and, by the grace of God, completed it successfully in April 2013. It was a challenging journey filled with fear, pain, and uncertainty, but also one of faith, strength, and hope. Today, I am proud to say that I am living a healthy, active, and fulfilling life.

As a farmer, I go about my daily work with energy and joy. Cancer did not take away my strength — it renewed my determination to live purposefully.

The Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative is very close to my heart. I have been part of it from the very beginning, and I consider it my “baby.” Through BBCSI, I found encouragement, courage, and a sense of community. The organization gave me the platform to share my story, inspire others, and raise awareness about breast cancer.

Thanks to BBCSI, I was able to turn my experience into a message of hope — letting others know that breast cancer is not the end of life, but a call to fight, to believe, and to live again.

I am Hannah Mngohol Tiohn — a survivor, a farmer, and a proud voice for hope through BBCSI.
