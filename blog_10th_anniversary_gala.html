10th Anniversary Gala

Countdown to the BBCSI 10th Anniversary Gala: RSVP Now!

"A decade of impact deserves an extraordinary celebration."

Published: 3 July 2026 · BBCSI Communications Team

A decade of impact merits a celebration to remember.

This year marks the 10th Anniversary of BBCSI — a milestone that reflects ten years of vision, collaboration, growth, and meaningful contributions to our community. What began as a shared commitment to making a difference has grown into a thriving network of members, partners, supporters, and friends who continue to shape our journey.

To commemorate this milestone, we are thrilled to invite you to join us for the BBCSI 10th Anniversary Gala Night — an unforgettable evening celebrating our past, honoring the people who made it possible, and looking ahead to the future we will build together.

What the Gala Will Feature
  • An evening of celebration with members, partners, sponsors, and distinguished guests
  • Recognition of individuals and organizations that have helped shape BBCSI's success
  • Special entertainment, fashion show, fundraising and memorable moments
  • Opportunities to reconnect with familiar faces and build new relationships
  • A look ahead at the exciting vision for BBCSI's next decade

Whether you have been part of our journey since the beginning or have joined us more recently, this celebration is about recognizing the collective impact we all made together.

Reserve Your Seat Today!

We are pleased to announce that RSVP registration is now officially open. We encourage you to reserve your spot as early as possible to ensure you don't miss this landmark celebration. Seating is limited, and we look forward to celebrating this important milestone with everyone who has helped make BBCSI's first ten years such a remarkable success.

Visit our RSVP page today to secure your attendance and be part of this historic evening.

Thank you for being part of the BBCSI story. We can't wait to celebrate ten incredible years with you — and to begin the next chapter together.

Here's to the past, the present, and the future of BBCSI.
