About Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative

We exist to break the stigma around breast cancer in Benue State, provide accurate information, link women to screening and treatment, and offer financial and emotional support to those who need it most.

Our Mission

Our Mission

To educate communities across Benue State about early detection, to support patients and survivors with financial and psycho-social services, and to advocate for better access to care.

Our Vision

Every woman in Benue State lives in a community that recognizes breast cancer early and supports her through diagnosis, treatment, and recovery.

Our Values

  • Compassion: We meet women where they are and provide non-judgmental care.
  • Truth: We replace myths with accurate, accessible information.
  • Collaboration: We partner with health workers, community leaders and donors.
  • Transparency: We steward resources responsibly and openly.

Our Story

BBCSI grew from conversations among survivors, caregivers and health workers who were tired of silence and harmful myths preventing women from seeking care. Since then, we've run awareness campaigns, screening drives, support groups and a small treatment loan program — all tailored to local realities across Benue.

We focus on community-led outreach: church and market talks, radio programs in local languages, and partnerships with clinics to expand screening. When a woman is diagnosed, we help her navigate care and, where needed, provide financial assistance so treatment isn't interrupted because of cost.

Impact at a glance

  • 2000+ people reached through outreach events
  • 150+ women supported with counseling and referrals
  • 50+ treatment loans provided to date
  • 10+ health facility partnerships strengthened

How We Work

A mix of education, direct support, and collaboration helps us prevent late-stage diagnoses and keep women in treatment.

Awareness

Community talks, radio and church outreach to change beliefs and encourage screening.

Support Groups

Peer-led groups where patients and survivors share experiences and practical advice.

Treatment Loans

Low-interest loans to help women complete prescribed treatment without financial interruption.

Partnerships

Working with clinics, labs and local leaders to improve screening and referral pathways.
