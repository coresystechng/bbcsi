About Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative

We exist to break the stigma around breast cancer in Benue State, and provide support to those who need it most.

Learn More

Our Story

BBCSI grew from conversations among survivors, caregivers and health workers who were tired of silence and harmful myths surrounding breast cancer. Since then, we have run awareness campaigns, screening drives, support groups and a small treatment loan program — all tailored to women living in Benue state.

Read Our Founder's Breast Cancer Story

Too often, a diagnosis is hidden for fear of gossip, discrimination, or beliefs that breast cancer is caused by witchcraft or spiritual attacks. We offer education, support, and practical help so that no woman fights alone. Together, we can replace myths with understanding, and secrecy with strength.

Our Vision

To dismantle the culture of fear, myth, secrecy and silence surrounding breast cancer among women of African descent.

Our Mission

To engage in advocacy, encourage regular breast self-exams and offer support to members actively undergoing care.

How We Work

A mix of education, direct support, and collaboration helps us prevent late-stage diagnoses and keep women in treatment.

Awareness

Community talks, radio and church outreach to change beliefs and encourage screening.

Support Groups

Peer-led groups where patients and survivors share experiences and practical advice.

Treatment Loans

Low-interest loans to help women complete prescribed treatment without financial interruption.

Partnerships

Working with clinics, labs and local leaders to improve screening and referral pathways.
