10th Anniversary Gala

A decade of impact, merits a celebration to remember.

"What began as a shared commitment to making a difference has grown into a thriving network of members, partners, supporters, and friends who continue to shape our journey."

Published: 1 july 2026· Author: Prof. (Emerita) Stevina Evuleocha-Ugbah, Founder, BBCSI

On this International Women's Day, we pause to celebrate the strength, resilience, and remarkable contributions of women across the globe. Women continue to inspire progress in their families, communities, industries, and nations, shaping a future that is more inclusive, innovative, and compassionate.

Empowering Women: A Cornerstone of Sustainable Development

At the Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative (BBCSI), we recognize that empowering women is not only a matter of equality, but also essential for sustainable development and meaningful societal advancement. Every woman who leads, innovates, mentors, and uplifts others becomes a powerful force for positive change.

Every woman who leads, innovates, mentors, and uplifts others becomes a powerful force for positive change.
Prof. (Emerita) Stevina Evuleocha-Ugbah, Founder, BBCSI
Honouring Women Who Break Barriers

Today, we honor the achievements of women who break barriers, challenge norms, and open doors for future generations. We also reaffirm our commitment to supporting environments where women can learn, grow, lead, and succeed without limitations.

A Call to Continued Action

Let this day remind us that progress happens when opportunities are shared, voices are heard, and leadership is inclusive. Together, we must continue to advocate for respect, dignity, and equal opportunity for women everywhere.

Let us continue to inspire inclusion, celebrate achievements, and build a future where every woman can thrive and ultimately succeed.

On behalf of BBCSI, we extend heartfelt appreciation to all the incredible women whose dedication and determination continue to shape a better world.

Happy International Women's Day!

With deep admiration and resolve,
Prof. (Emerita) Stevina Evuleocha-Ugbah
Founder, BBCSI
