BBCSI Celebrates Director Mary Ahua on Her Milestone 70th Birthday!

“Seventy is not just a number; it is a testament to a life of experience, wisdom, service, compassion, and impact.”

Published: 30 August 2026 · BBCSI Communications Team

Today, we join throngs of well-wishers to celebrate a remarkable woman—Mrs. Mary Ahua, as she ascends to the 7th floor.

Seventy is not just a number; it is a testament to a life of experience, wisdom, service, compassion, and impact. Mrs. Ahua has certainly made her years count.

As Chairperson of the Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative Advisory Board, she has, through her service, helped strengthen a message of hope: that no one facing breast cancer should feel alone, forgotten, or without support.

“Every day of her life has been marked by generosity of spirit, commitment to others, and a steadfast willingness to serve.”
Prof. Stevina Evuleocha-Ugbah
Founder, BBCSI
A Legacy of Purpose and Compassion

But beyond the title and the position, we celebrate the woman whose generosity of spirit, commitment to others, and willingness to serve has made a difference in her community.

Mrs. Ahua, at 70, you have every reason to look back with gratitude and forward with renewed hope and joy. May this new decade bring you abundant health, peace, laughter, fulfillment, and many more opportunities to inspire those around you.

A Life Worth Celebrating

We are excited to celebrate 70 remarkable years of Mrs. Mary Ahua; to a life of service, compassion, and leadership; and to many more glorious years ahead.

Happy 70th birthday, Mrs. Mary Ahua! May your light continue to shine bright, inspire, and make a difference.

Happy Birthday from all your friends at BBCSI!

With love and celebration,
The Team at BBCSI
