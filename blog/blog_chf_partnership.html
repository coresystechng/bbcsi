BBCSI Partners with Nigerian Cancer Health Fund to Support Breast Cancer Patients

Published: 25 October 2025 · Author: BBCSI

In a significant development for cancer care in Nigeria, the Nigerian Cancer Health Fund (CHF) continues to make remarkable strides in making cancer treatments more accessible and affordable for underprivileged populations. Through a groundbreaking public-private partnership led by Roche, the International Finance Corporation (IFC), and Nigerian health authorities, the Fund is transforming the landscape of cancer care in the country.

The Fund provides financial support of up to ₦2 million (approximately USD 4,865) for treatment costs including medication, chemotherapy, and radiation therapy.

Measurable Impact

The impact of the Fund has been substantial and measurable. By 2024, over ₦1.4 billion had been invested in treating approximately 1,400 patients, with more than 3,600 patients registered in its programs. The initiative focuses on three of the most prevalent cancers in Nigeria:

  • Breast Cancer
  • Cervical Cancer
  • Prostate Cancer
Strengthening National Cancer Care

Beyond individual patient care, the CHF is actively working to strengthen the national cancer care system through multiple initiatives:

  • Improving the quality of services
  • Working through public health facilities
  • Partnering with the national health insurance scheme
  • Reducing out-of-pocket costs for patients
BBCSI's Partnership

The Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative (BBCSI) is proud to announce its partnership with the Cancer Health Fund to bring vital assistance to breast cancer patients in Benue State. This collaboration will help ensure that more patients in our community have access to the life-saving treatments they need.

Together, we're making cancer care more accessible for all Nigerians.
