Delegates Arrive in Makurdi as the 10th Anniversary Week Celebration Kicks Off

Monday, 12 October 2026 ·
BBCSI Communications Team

The opening day witnessed the arrival of delegates from Adikpo, Gboko, Konshisha and Otukpo, who converged on Makurdi, the Benue State capital, to participate in this historic milestone celebration.

Their arrival marked the beginning of what promises to be a memorable anniversary gathering, bringing together members, supporters and advocates united by a common purpose: raising awareness about breast cancer, supporting survivors and strengthening the campaign for early detection and improved access to care.

A Warm Welcome and Comfortable Accommodation

To ensure a smooth and comfortable experience for the visiting delegates, accommodation arrangements were made at Zaginas Suite, No. 2 Jumbo Lane, Hudco Quarters, Makurdi.

This thoughtful hospitality was made possible through the generous sponsorship of Madam Mary Ahua, Chairperson of the BBCSI Advisory Board. Her support reflects the spirit of generosity, solidarity and collective responsibility that has continued to sustain the work and vision of BBCSI over the years.

By providing this essential support, Madam Ahua demonstrated her commitment to the success of the anniversary celebration and the welfare of the delegates who travelled from different parts of Benue State to be part of this landmark occasion.

Ten Years of Making a Difference

The 10th anniversary of BBCSI represents more than just a celebration of longevity. It is a moment to reflect on a decade of advocacy, awareness creation, encouragement, education and support for individuals and families affected by breast cancer.

Over the years, the initiative has remained committed to promoting breast cancer awareness, encouraging early detection, offering support to those affected and helping to break the stigma and misconceptions surrounding the disease. The gathering of delegates from different communities across Benue State further demonstrates the importance of grassroots participation in advancing the breast cancer awareness campaign.

A Celebration of Commitment, Unity and Hope

As the delegates settled into their accommodation in Makurdi, the atmosphere was one of anticipation, renewed commitment and excitement. The arrival of participants from Adikpo, Gboko, Konshisha and Otukpo underscores the growing spirit of unity among BBCSI members and the shared determination to sustain the organisation’s mission.

The first day may have centred largely on arrivals and accommodation, but it carried a deeper message: the strength of an organisation lies not only in its years of existence but also in the people who believe in its vision and continue to champion its cause.

As BBCSI celebrates ten remarkable years, the anniversary provides an opportunity to honour the journey so far, appreciate those who have contributed to its growth and renew the commitment to a future where breast cancer awareness, early detection, support and access to care remain priorities. With the delegates arriving in Makurdi, the stage was set for the activities ahead.
