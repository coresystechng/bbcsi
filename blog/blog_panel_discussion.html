Thriving After Breast Cancer: Insights from BBCSI's Expert Panel

Published: 28 October 2025 · Author: BBCSI

The journey beyond a breast cancer diagnosis is often filled with both fear and uncertainty. On Sunday, October 26, 2025, the Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative (BBCSI) hosted an enlightening online panel discussion that brought together distinguished experts to share valuable insights on thriving after breast cancer and managing the fear of recurrence.

Our Distinguished Panelists
  • Prof. (Emerita) Stevina Evuleocha-Ugbah - Breast Cancer Survivor
  • Dr. Monica Hemen-Eimunjeze - Former Policy Maker
  • Dr. Iorbo Alex Tyover - Breast Cancer Surgeon
Embracing Life Beyond Diagnosis

The panelists emphasized that life after diagnosis presents an opportunity for rebuilding and self-discovery. While physical and emotional changes are part of the journey, they also bring chances for new beginnings. The panel highlighted how support groups play a crucial role in recovery, offering:

  • A safe space for sharing experiences
  • Reduction in feelings of isolation
  • Inspiration and courage from fellow survivors
  • Opportunities to heal together
Managing the Fear of Recurrence

The experts acknowledged that fear of cancer returning is a natural part of the recovery journey. They provided practical strategies for managing this concern:

  • Regular medical checkups
  • Mindfulness practices
  • Physical activity
  • Professional counseling

Survivors were encouraged to focus on aspects within their control, such as self-care, maintaining healthy habits, and nurturing positive relationships.

Building a Supportive Community

The panel concluded with an empowering message about collective strength and hope. Thriving after breast cancer isn't a solitary journey—it flourishes in community. BBCSI remains dedicated to creating an environment where survivors feel seen, supported, and empowered through:

  • Continuous awareness programs
  • Counseling services
  • Community outreach
  • Survivor support networks
