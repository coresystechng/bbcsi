BBCSI Founder Pays Courtesy Visit to Office of the First Lady, Benue State, to Explore Collaboration on Breast Cancer Awareness

Published: 13 October 2026 · Makurdi, Benue State · BBCSI Communications Team

The Founder of the Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative (BBCSI), Prof. Stevina Ugba, paid a courtesy visit to the Coordinator, Office of the First Lady, Benue State, Her Excellency, Hon. Mrs. Scholastica Terngu Ben-Sor, to explore strategic areas of collaboration aimed at advancing breast cancer awareness, early detection, patient support, and access to essential healthcare services.

The proposed collaboration aligns with the Office of the First Lady’s commitment to promoting the health, well-being, and social protection of women and vulnerable groups through strategic partnerships and people-centred initiatives.

Areas of Collaboration

The discussions focused on strategic areas of collaboration to advance:

  • Breast cancer awareness
  • Early detection
  • Patient support
  • Access to essential healthcare services

Through collaborative efforts with relevant organisations and stakeholders, the Office seeks to encourage greater awareness, facilitate access to preventive healthcare information, and contribute to building a healthier and more informed society.

The visit is part of BBCSI’s wider effort during its 10th anniversary year to build institutional partnerships across Benue State. See also: BBCSI Seeks Collaboration with Benue Ministry of Women Affairs.

Source: Demesugh Yaji, Chief Press Secretary to the Coordinator, Office of the First Lady, Benue State.
