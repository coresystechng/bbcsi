Our Programs

We run community-led initiatives that increase awareness, improve early detection and support women through treatment.

Learn More

Core Programs

Each program is designed to work with local realities — trusted messengers, low-cost solutions, and strong referral pathways to care.

Support Groups & Survivorship

We provide emotional support, practical advice and a safe space to share experiences. We run monthly meetings in Gboko and outreach branches in neighbouring LGAs.

Awareness & Community Outreach

Church and market talks, radio segments in local languages, and school programs that teach signs of breast cancer and the importance of early presentation.

Treatment Loans & Patient Support

Low-interest loans to help members complete prescribed treatment without financial interruption. Contact us for more info.

Partnerships & Health System Strengthening

We work with hospitals and local leaders to increase screening access, strengthen referral pathways and train health workers in patient-centred care.
