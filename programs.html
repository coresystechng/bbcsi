Our Programs

We run community-led initiatives that increase awareness, improve early detection and support women through treatment.

Core Programs

Each program is designed to work with local realities — trusted messengers, low-cost solutions, and strong referral pathways to care.

Support Groups & Survivorship

Peer-led groups provide emotional support, practical advice and a safe space to share experiences. We run monthly meetings in Gboko and outreach branches in neighbouring LGAs.

How to join: Contact us or drop in at the next meeting. Transport support available for patients.

Awareness & Community Outreach

Church and market talks, radio segments in local languages, and school programs that teach signs of breast cancer and the importance of early presentation.

Reach: Over 2,000 people reached yearly through targeted campaigns.

Treatment Loans & Patient Support

Interest-subsidized loans and emergency grants to ensure women complete their prescribed treatment without interruption due to cost.

Apply: Use the Apply Loan page or contact our office for eligibility and required documents.

Partnerships & Health System Strengthening

We work with hospitals, labs and local leaders to increase screening access, strengthen referral pathways and train health workers in patient-centred care.

Partner: Email Benuecancergroup@gmail.com to discuss collaboration.

Screening & Training

Community Screening Days

Regular screening days in partnership clinics offer clinical breast exams and referrals for diagnostic imaging when needed. We prioritize outreach in rural and underserved communities.

Next screening: Check our Get Involved events or contact us for dates and locations.

Volunteer & Health Worker Training

Orientation and role-specific training for volunteers, community health workers and clinicians on breast awareness, counselling skills and referral processes.

Interested in training: Email our team or sign up via the volunteer form.
