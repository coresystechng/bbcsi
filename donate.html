Why Give?

Late detection and financial barriers make breast cancer deadly for many women in Benue. By donating, you help us find cancer earlier, keep women in treatment, and change harmful beliefs through community education.

NGN 5,000

Covers transport for a woman to attend a follow-up appointment.

NGN 20,000

Helps subsidize diagnostic tests for one patient.

NGN 50,000

Supports community screening for 30 people.
