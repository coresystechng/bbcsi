Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative (BBCSI) Awareness Campaign 2025

Published: 11 October 2025 · Author: BBCSI

Every October, the world unites to raise awareness about breast cancer — a disease that affects millions of women and families globally. In Benue State, the Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative (BBCSI) is taking this mission to heart with a powerful call to action: join the campaign to save lives!

The campaign aims to spread vital information about breast cancer prevention, early detection, and support systems for those affected. Through education, advocacy, and community participation, BBCSI continues to empower women and men to take charge of their health and support loved ones in the fight against cancer.

Event Details
  • Date: Saturday, 18 October 2025
  • Time: 08:00 AM Prompt
  • Venue: Christ the King Catholic Church, Vandeikya, Benue State

About BBCSI

The Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative (BBCSI) is dedicated to promoting breast cancer awareness, offering support to patients and survivors, and fostering a culture of early screening and health education across Benue State. Their ongoing campaigns continue to bring hope, healing, and unity in the fight against cancer.
