From Silence to Strength.

In many Benue communities, women face fear and stigma after a breast cancer diagnosis. Treatment costs are often out of reach, and worse still, diagnosis is hidden for fear of gossip or the dangerous belief that cancer is caused by witchcraft or spiritual attacks.

We stand with every woman in Benue, offering education, emotional support, and practical financial help. We bring truth, hope, and resources so that no woman fights alone. Read Our Full Mission →

What We Do

Since its inception, BBCSI has organized in-person awareness campaigns every October in different local governments of Benue State. To date, our programs include:

Support Group

A safe, welcoming space for women and their families to share experiences and find strength together.

Learn More
Awareness & Outreach

Church visits, market outreaches, television and radio talks that spread information on early detection and treatment.

View Events
Treatment Loans

Low-interest, long-term loans for members actively undergoing treatment, easing the financial burden.

Get Assistance
Community Partnerships

Collaboration with hospitals and local leaders to expand screening access and strengthen support networks.
