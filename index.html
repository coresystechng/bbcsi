From Silence to Strength.

In many Benue communities, women face fear and stigma after a breast cancer diagnosis. Treatment costs are often out of reach, and worse still, diagnosis is hidden for fear of gossip or the dangerous belief that cancer is caused by witchcraft or spiritual attacks.

We stand with every woman in Benue, offering education, emotional support, and practical financial help. We bring truth, hope, and resources so that no woman fights alone. Read Our Full Mission

What We Do

Since its inception, BBCSI has organized in-person awareness campaigns every October in different local governments of Benue State.

Support Groups

A safe, welcoming space for women
to share experiences and find strength together.

Awareness & Outreach

Church visits, market outreaches, television and radio talks
that spread information on early detection and treatment.

Treatment Loans

Low-interest, long-term loans for members actively
undergoing treatment, easing the financial burden.

Community Partnerhip

Collaboration with hospitals and local leaders to expand
screening access and strengthen support networks.

Breast cancer is not a curse. It is a disease we can fight together with knowledge, early detection, and proper medical care.
