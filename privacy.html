Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2025

Introduction

Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative ("BBCSI", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal information through our website and related services (the "Site").

1. Personal information we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, phone) when you contact us, sign up or donate.
  • Information included in messages you send (form submissions, emails).
  • Usage data collected automatically (IP address, browser, pages visited) via analytics tools.
  • Payment information processed by third-party payment providers (we do not store full card details on our servers).

2. How we use personal information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide the services you request.
  • Manage donations, issue receipts and communicate about your support.
  • Send newsletters or updates if you opt-in.
  • Improve and secure the Site, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing and disclosure

We may share personal data with:

  • Service providers who help with payments, hosting, analytics, email and outreach — only to the extent necessary.
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by law.

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand how people use the Site and to improve it. Cookies are small files stored on your device; you can disable them in your browser but some features may not work correctly.

5. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy or to comply with legal obligations. Donation records may be retained for accounting and audit purposes.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access, but no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure. If you believe your interaction with the Site is no longer secure, please contact us immediately.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by us. To exercise these rights contact us at Benuecancergroup@gmail.com. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

8. International transfers

Personal data may be transferred to countries outside your jurisdiction for processing by service providers. When we do so we will take steps to ensure appropriate protections are in place.

9. Children

Our Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have unintentionally received data about a child, contact us to have it removed.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact us at Benuecancergroup@gmail.com or by post to the address on our Site.
