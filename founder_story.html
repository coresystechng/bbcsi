A Journey of Survival and Service: Prof. (Emerita) Stevina Evuleocha-Ugbah

Published: 10 October 2025 · Author: BBCSI

Prof. (Emerita) Stevina Evuleocha-Ugbah is a retired Professor of Marketing & Entrepreneurship of the California State University, East Bay in Hayward, California. A breast cancer survivor herself, Prof. Stevina's lived experience became the catalyst for a life of focused service and advocacy.

“I saw how a single bill, a single window of payment, could change everything — and I knew I had to act.”

In 2013, after undergoing radiation treatment, Stevina received a bill totalling $38,000. At the payment window she learned that her insurance covered most of that amount and that she only needed to pay $15 out of pocket. That moment crystallised for her the inequality and hidden burdens many women face—especially in places where health systems and insurance support are limited or non-existent.

Three years after her diagnosis, when the opportunity arose to return to Benue State, Nigeria on a medical mission, she knew instinctively that she would seek out women who had experienced breast cancer. In 2016 she organized an initial support group of 12 women and challenged them to go out and share the message that breast cancer is not a death sentence.

What began as a circle of 12 women has, over nine years, grown into the Benue Breast Cancer Support Initiative — a community of survivors, caregivers, health professionals and allies committed to replacing fatalism with early detection, truth, and practical support.

Prof. Stevina's approach combines lived experience, academic rigor, and grassroots organizing. Her work emphasises empathy, sustainable support, and community-led solutions: support groups that empower survivors; treatment loan programs that reduce financial barriers; and outreach that combats harmful myths about breast cancer.

This story is shared in tribute to the resilience of survivors and the generosity of volunteers and donors who make BBCSI's work possible.
